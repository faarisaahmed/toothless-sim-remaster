Music for Night Alone (dragon-flying-sim)
=========================================

All six tracks are original compositions written for this game. No melody,
motif or arrangement is taken from any existing tune or film score.

    emberwing-title.mp3    "Emberwing (Title)"   — title screen
    the-hearth.mp3         "The Hearth"          — the prologue
    emberwing-flight.mp3   "Emberwing (Flight)"  — the archipelago
    emberwing-jig.mp3      "Emberwing Jig"       — flying flat out
    emberwing-raid.mp3     "Emberwing (Raid)"    — the raid
    held-breath.mp3        "Held Breath"         — being seen

They are generated from source: the notes are in tools/music/score.py and
`python3 tools/music/build.py` rebuilds every file here.

Instruments
-----------
Rendered with the GeneralUser GS SoundFont bank by S. Christian Collins,
https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS — GeneralUser GS License v2.0:
"You may use GeneralUser GS without restriction for your own music creation,
private or commercial." Attribution is not required by that licence; it is
given here anyway.

Encoded as 160 kbps CBR MP3 (constant bit rate keeps seeking exact, which the
seamless loop in js/audio.js relies on).
