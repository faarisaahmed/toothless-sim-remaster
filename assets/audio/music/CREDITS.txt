Music for Night Alone (dragon-flying-sim)
=========================================

All six tracks are original compositions written for this game. No melody,
motif or arrangement is taken from any existing tune or film score.

    emberwing-title.mp3    "Emberwing (Title)"   — title screen
    the-hearth.mp3         "The Hearth"          — the prologue
    emberwing-flight.mp3   "Emberwing (Flight)"  — the archipelago
    emberwing-jig.mp3      "Emberwing Jig"       — flying flat out
    emberwing-raid.mp3     "Emberwing (Raid)"    — the raid
    held-breath.mp3        "Held Breath"         — being seen

They are generated from source: the notes are in tools/music/score.py and
`python3 tools/music/build.py` rebuilds every file here.

Instruments
-----------
Most parts are played on recordings of real instruments from two public-domain
sample libraries by Sam Gossner / Versilian Studios LLC. Both are released
under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
"you can do whatever you want with these sounds (even make commercial
software), no royalties, no credit, no special terms." Credit is given here
anyway.

  VSCO 2 Community Edition — https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
  (commit 440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e), CC0 1.0:
    fiddle ............ Solo Violin (arco vibrato; spiccato for very short notes)
    strings, fstrings,  Violin, Viola and Cello Sections (sustain vibrato,
    trem, pizz ........ spiccato, tremolo, pizzicato), with the Solo Violin
                        doubling the section melody
    cello ............. Cello Section
    contrabass ........ Solo Contrabass (sustain, spiccato)
    horn, horns ....... F Horn (the section played as two horns)
    brass ............. Trumpet doubled by F Horn
    trombone, tuba .... Tenor Trombone (two players), Tuba
    timpani ........... Timpani
    bass drum ......... VSCO 1 Percussion bass drum (muted strokes)

  Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) — https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
  (commit c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e), CC0 1.0:
    whistle ........... Baroque Tenor, Alto and Soprano Recorders (sustain)
    harp .............. Folk Harp
    bodhran ........... Frame Drum (large and small, open and muted strokes)
    taiko ............. Concert Bass Drum 2, tuned up, with the large Frame Drum
    guitar ............ Strumstick (fingerpicked)
    dulcimer .......... Psaltery (plucked)
    tambourine ........ Tambourine 1
    glass ............. Wine Glasses (bowed), over the soundfont's bowed glass

The remaining parts — the solo voice (the kulning call), the choir and "oohs",
the bagpipes, the shakuhachi and the bowed-glass pad under the wine glasses —
have no freely licensed recording, and are rendered with the GeneralUser GS
SoundFont bank by S. Christian Collins, https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS
— GeneralUser GS License v2.0: "You may use GeneralUser GS without restriction
for your own music creation, private or commercial." Attribution is not
required by that licence; it is given here anyway. Every recorded instrument
is also level-matched against the GeneralUser preset it replaced, so the
balance the score was written for carries over.

None of these samples are redistributed in this repository: the build fetches
them (tools/music/libraries.py). Only the rendered MP3s ship.

Mixing: a sampler and mix chain written for this game (tools/music/sampler.py,
engine.py): per-instrument articulation choice, velocity crossfades, legato
and portamento, vibrato, section doubling, orchestral seating, synthetic hall
and room impulse responses, EQ, bus compression and limiting.

Encoded as 160 kbps CBR MP3 (constant bit rate keeps seeking exact, which the
seamless loop in js/audio.js relies on).
